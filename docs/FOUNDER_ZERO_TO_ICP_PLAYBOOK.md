# Founder Playbook: Zero to ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

> **For technical founders and builders who built a product but don't know who to sell to, what to do next, or how to get their first 5 customers.**

---

## 1. What is an ICP (And Why Most Advice is Wrong)?

An **ICP** (Ideal Customer Profile) is **not**:
- A fictional demographic persona ("Marketing Mary, age 32, drinks oat lattes").
- "Any business with a database or API."
- "Small businesses and startups."

An **ICP is**:
> **A specific human being with a specific job title, who suffers an acute operational migraine every single week, has corporate credit card authority to pay for a solution, and will buy your product in 7 days without a 6-month enterprise sales committee.**

---

## 2. Why Founders Fail When Launching a Product

When you spend 6 months coding a product, your natural impulse is to talk about everything you built:
- *"It has a real-time visual canvas, database connectors, automated webhook triggers, role-based access control, export formats, and custom transformers!"*

**What happens:**
1. The prospect gets overwhelmed.
2. They say: *"Sounds really cool, good luck with the launch!"*
3. They never reply again, and you never make a dime.

**The Golden Rule:**
> **Prospects do not buy "platforms". They buy an aspirin for the exact headache that woke them up this morning.**

---

## 3. The 6-Step Discovery Roadmap

```
[01] Product Inventory ──▶ [02] 3 Audiences ──▶ [03] 1-Feature Wedge ──▶ [04] Source 15 People ──▶ [05] 45-Word Touch ──▶ [06] 15-Min Call & Decision
```

---

### Step 01: The Honest Feature Dump (Catalog)
**The Goal:** Unbundle your software monolith into isolated parts.
- List 4 to 8 distinct features you have built.
- Be honest about which features are production-ready versus half-baked.
- *Why:* You cannot choose a razor-sharp weapon until you lay all your tools on the workbench.

---

### Step 02: Brainstorm 2–3 Candidate Audiences
**The Goal:** Never bet your company's survival on a single guess.
- Use this formula: `[Specific Role] at [Company Type] suffering from [Daily Agony]`.
- Identify their current **ugly workaround**: Are they copy-pasting data into Google Sheets? Running fragile bash cron jobs? Paging engineers on weekends?
- Contrast 3 different cohorts (e.g., FinTech Compliance Leads vs. Solo Founders vs. Enterprise Data Engineers).

---

### Step 03: The 1-Feature Wedge (De-Engineering)
**The Goal:** Pick ONE feature to pitch, and forbid the other 9.
- A wedge is the sharp point of an axe that splits the log. Your entire codebase is the log.
- For each audience, select the **single feature** that directly cures their headache.
- **Strict Rule:** Mark all other features in your product as **FORBIDDEN TO MENTION** on initial calls. If you start listing other features, the prospect tunes out.

---

### Step 04: Source 15 Real Human Beings
**The Goal:** You do not need 5,000 scraped emails. You need 15 targeted people.
- **Where to look:**
  - **LinkedIn:** Search `"Target Job Title"` + filter by Company Size: 20–200 employees.
  - **GitHub / Issue Trackers:** Search for people filing issues or commenting on competitor open-source tools.
  - **Practitioner Communities:** Subreddits, Discord servers, and Slack groups where people ask "How do you all handle [Problem]?"
- Add 15 real names and emails/LinkedIn profiles into your discovery pipeline.

---

### Step 05: The 45-Word Peer Curiosity Message
**The Goal:** Get a 25–40% reply rate without sales pitch rejection.
- **The Mom Test Rule:** Never pitch your product. Never ask for a 15-minute demo. Never send a link or Calendly invite.
- Ask an authentic practitioner question about how they handle the friction today.

#### Copy-Paste Template:
```text
Subject: Quick question about audit logs at {{company}}

Hi {{firstName}},

Saw you lead compliance at {{company}}.

Quick question: when webhooks fail during customer reconciliations, are you guys tracking failures in spreadsheets or did you build an internal replay queue?

Doing some research on this workflow friction—curious how your team handles it.

Best,
{{your_name}}
```

- When they reply, Quick ICP **automatically kills all scheduled follow-ups** so you can talk to them like a real human being.

---

### Step 06: The 15-Minute Discovery Call & Kill/Double-Down Decision
**The Goal:** Uncover objective truth, not polite compliments.

#### Do NOT:
- Open your laptop or screenshare a demo.
- Ask *"Would you buy something like this?"* (Everyone says yes to be nice; nobody actually pays).

#### The 5 Diagnostic Questions to Ask:
1. *"Walk me through the last time [Problem] happened?"*
2. *"What did you do to fix it? How much time or money did that cost your team?"*
3. *"What other solutions or tools have you tried in the past? Why didn't you stick with them?"*
4. *"Who else on your team gets pulled in when this breaks?"*
5. *"If this was solved by next week, what is the exact procurement process to pilot it?"*

#### The Decision Rule:
- **Kill the Cohort:** If 10–15 people say *"It's slightly annoying, but not a priority"* or have never spent a dollar to fix it, **kill the hypothesis**. You did not fail—you just saved 6 months of wasted runway.
- **Double Down:** If multiple prospects say *"Can I buy this right now? Here is my company credit card,"* you have found your **ICP**.
